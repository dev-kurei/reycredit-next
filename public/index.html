Loan data health
Checks that each loan's amortization agrees with its terms and total payable.
A common slip is typing the total of all instalments into the monthly amortization field. The schedule then bills that amount every month, producing balances many times larger than the loan itself. This finds those loans and shows what each figure should be. Nothing is changed until you choose to apply it.
